# Community adapters

Community adapters are adapter plugins contributed and maintained by members of the community. We welcome and encourage [adapter plugins contributions](./contribute-core-adapters.md#contribute-to-a-pre-existing-adapter) from the dbt community. Please be mindful that these [community maintainers](./connect-adapters.md#maintainers) are intrepid volunteers who donate their time and effort — so be kind, understanding, and help out where you can!

In v2, [DuckDB](./local/connect-data-platform/duckdb-setup.md) is available as a community adapter. There are also several trusted adapters available: Apache Spark, BigQuery, Databricks, DuckDB, Redshift, Snowflake. For the full list, refer to [trusted adapters](./trusted-adapters.md?version=2.0).

If you'd like to contribute a community adapter for v2, refer to [creating a Fusion adapter](../guides/adapter-creation-v2.md?step=1).
